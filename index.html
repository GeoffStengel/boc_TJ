Warnings on the reference label

Contains caffeine and other stimulants. The reference label warns against use by anyone under 18 or anyone who is pregnant or nursing. It also warns against combining the formula with other sources of caffeine or stimulants.

The label advises consulting a healthcare professional if you have a medical condition or take medication and discontinuing use if you experience adverse effects.
