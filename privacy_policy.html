Privacy Policy

The policy, effective date, and business contact details are awaiting approval.

This draft describes how the operator of the 007-NRG website ("we" or "us") handles information. It also describes proposed practices for services that may be added later.

1. Information we collect

This website has no contact form or checkout. The information we may handle depends on how you interact with us.

  • Your name, email address, or message if you contact us separately.
  • Order and delivery information if online checkout is enabled in the future.
  • Technical information processed by our website hosting and service providers, such as IP addresses, browser information, and request logs.

2. Data saved in your browser

The meal planner and food tracker use your browser's local storage to save your last meal plan, food log, and the tracker's net-carb goal. These tools do not send those entries to a server. Clearing this website's data in your browser removes the saved entries.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Operate and maintain our website.
  • Respond to customer inquiries.
  • Process orders and provide customer support if purchasing becomes available.
  • Investigate security incidents, fraud, or misuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Website hosting and external services

The website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information to serve pages. The site has no analytics integration.

Pages request fonts from Google Fonts. If Google Reviews links are enabled, following them takes you to Google. External services handle information under their own privacy policies.

If we introduce a payment processor, shipping service, analytics platform, or shared review service, we will update this policy to describe the relevant information practices.

5. Payment information

Online checkout is unavailable. This website does not collect payment card details.

If checkout is activated, payments may be processed by a third-party provider. We will identify the provider and update this policy before collecting payment information.

6. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. We may share information with service providers when necessary to operate our business, fulfill orders, respond to lawful requests, or protect legal rights.

Any additional disclosures required by applicable privacy law will be provided as necessary.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website, electronic transmission, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to exercise other rights provided by applicable law.

We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a privacy request. See contact information for the status of the privacy contact.

9. Children's privacy

This website and its products are intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal obligations. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
