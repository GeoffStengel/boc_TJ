Proposed change-of-mind policy

Due to the nature of our products, we do not offer discretionary returns, exchanges, or refunds for change of mind, subject to applicable consumer protection laws.

1. No change-of-mind returns

Except where required by law or expressly authorized by us, purchases are final.

We do not accept discretionary returns or exchanges for reasons including:

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Ordering an unwanted flavor or quantity.
  • Personal taste or flavor preferences.
  • Dissatisfaction with subjective or individually experienced results.
  • Products opened or used after delivery, unless a legal remedy applies.

2. Damaged, defective, or incorrect orders

If you receive a damaged, defective, incorrectly shipped, or otherwise nonconforming product, see contact information.

Please provide your order number, a description of the issue, and photographs if available.

We will review the request and provide any refund, replacement, or other remedy required by applicable law.

3. Product safety concerns

If you suspect a product is contaminated, improperly sealed, mislabeled, or otherwise unsafe, do not continue using it. Contact us with the product's lot information and a description of the concern.

Nothing in this policy limits rights or remedies relating to defective or unsafe products that cannot legally be excluded.

4. Cancellations

Orders may begin processing shortly after payment confirmation. Cancellation requests are not guaranteed once processing begins.

If we cannot fulfill an order, we will provide any refund required by applicable law. Customers also retain applicable statutory cancellation rights.

5. Refund processing

When a refund is approved or legally required, it will generally be issued through the original payment method where available.

Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider and financial institution.

6. Customer responsibility

Customers are responsible for reviewing product descriptions, ingredient information, warnings, quantities, and shipping details before completing a purchase.

Products must be used only in accordance with their lawful intended use and applicable instructions and warnings. Customers should seek qualified medical advice where appropriate.

This section does not exclude or reduce any legal obligations of the seller concerning product safety, labeling, fitness for purpose, or consumer rights.

7. No guaranteed results

Individual experiences may vary. We do not guarantee specific energy, performance, focus, endurance, or other outcomes from product use.

Any product claims must be supported and presented in accordance with applicable law.

8. Applicable law

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive or restrict any consumer right or legal remedy that cannot be waived or restricted under applicable law.

If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue to apply to the extent permitted by law.
