Shipping Policy

Proposed terms for future 007-NRG orders. Online ordering is unavailable. The policy and its effective date are awaiting approval.

This draft covers order processing, shipment, and delivery. Shipping destinations, rates, carriers, and processing schedules will be confirmed before purchases are accepted.

1. Shipping availability

Orders will be accepted only for destinations where shipment and sale of the products are permitted by applicable law and supported by our fulfillment services.

We reserve the right to decline or cancel an order when we cannot legally or reasonably fulfill it. Any payment collected for an unfulfilled canceled order will be refunded as required by applicable law.

2. Order processing

Once checkout is enabled, applicable processing estimates will be presented before purchase or included in the order confirmation.

Processing time and shipping transit time are separate. Weekends, holidays, inventory limitations, and verification requirements may affect fulfillment.

3. Shipping rates

Available shipping methods and charges will be disclosed before the customer completes payment. Charges may vary by destination, package weight, order size, and selected delivery service.

4. Delivery estimates

Any delivery estimates provided at checkout are estimates rather than guarantees, unless expressly identified as guaranteed.

If shipment is delayed, we will provide notices and any cancellation or refund options required by applicable law.

5. Shipping address accuracy

Customers are responsible for reviewing their shipping information before placing an order and promptly notifying us of errors.

Address changes may not be possible once an order has been processed or shipped. Additional shipping charges caused by an incorrect address may apply where permitted by law and disclosed to the customer.

6. Lost, damaged, or missing packages

For damaged, incorrect, or missing orders, see contact information.

Include the order number, a description of the issue, and photographs when relevant. We will review the situation and provide any remedy required under applicable law.

We are not responsible for delays or events outside our reasonable control to the extent permitted by law. This does not limit statutory rights concerning delivery, damaged goods, or non-delivery.

7. Delivery restrictions

Certain products may be subject to age, location, carrier, or other restrictions. We may require verification before processing an eligible order.

Customers must be at least 18 years old to purchase products offered through this storefront.

8. International shipping

International shipping is not currently confirmed. If introduced, available countries and any applicable customs, duties, taxes, or import restrictions will be disclosed before purchase.
